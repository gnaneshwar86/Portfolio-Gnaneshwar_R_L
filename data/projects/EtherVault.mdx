EtherVault is a blockchain-powered platform designed to verify the authenticity of academic certificates.

It eliminates fraud by storing certificate hashes on the blockchain, ensuring tamper-proof validation.

Built with Web3, React, and Node.js, it provides a decentralized approach to academic verification systems.
